WELCOME TO TOWKAI OS

This MVP is about seeing what all your AI agents are doing, not just generating tasks. TowkAI gives you one visual control room to monitor agent work across projects, read updates, and intervene when needed.

1. SEE EVERYTHING

The left panel lists projects and agents. Status labels show who is running, blocked, waiting for review, idle or done.

2. WATCH LIVE MOVEMENT

Pixel agents move to different rooms based on their activity: research, build, design, outreach, review or ops.

3. INSPECT AN AGENT

Click an agent to see its task, progress, recent updates and outputs. You can pause, resume, redirect or approve it.

4. HANDLE EXCEPTIONS

Anything blocked or waiting for approval appears in Founder Attention so the founder always knows where to act.
