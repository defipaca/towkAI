# Pitch notes

## 30-second pitch

TowkAI OS is a gamified AI agent IDE. As founders use more AI agents, work gets fragmented across chats and tools, and they lose visibility. TowkAI gives them one visual control room to see what all their agents are doing across projects, track progress, read recent updates, and intervene when needed. Pixel agents move based on activity, the right panel shows exactly what each agent is working on, and Founder Attention surfaces anything blocked or waiting for approval.

## 60-second demo script

This is TowkAI OS — a pixel-art control room for AI agents. On the left, I can see my projects and all active agents. In the centre, every pixel worker represents one AI agent, and it moves to different rooms depending on what kind of work it is doing. For example, Researcher is in the research bay, Builder is in the build lab, and anything needing my decision comes to Founder Review.

If I click an agent, the right-hand inspector shows its exact task, progress, recent updates and output. I can pause it, send it to review, unblock it, or approve it. At the bottom, the activity feed shows what changed recently, and Founder Attention surfaces the items I actually need to act on.

So the product is simple: instead of wondering what all your AI agents are doing across tools, TowkAI gives you one visible, controllable AI workforce dashboard.
